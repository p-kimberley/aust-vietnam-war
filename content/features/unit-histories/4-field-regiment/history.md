### 6 August 1967: Bunker, Operation Ballarat

*Significant* · Fire support for 7 RAR

Australian: 6 killed, 19 wounded. Enemy: 15 killed, 25 wounded.

Operation Ballarat, in the Hat Dich area. A Company, 7 RAR, had been inserted quietly to ambush trails to enemy camps, and its 2 Platoon killed two enemy who walked into a hasty ambush. Sent along the track after the rest of the enemy squad, the platoon ran into heavy automatic fire from a defended position held by a company of the 274th Regiment's 3rd Battalion, covering the withdrawal of its battalion from a camp nearby. The whole company was committed, supported by air strikes and by 106 Field Battery and a US battery, until the enemy withdrew after about four hours. Six Australians were killed and about twenty wounded; an estimated 15 enemy were killed and 25 wounded by the company, and many more by the artillery and air strikes.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=586) · Report's sources: Intsum, AWM95 1/4/55.pdf p172; AWM279 569/R4/66; On the Offensive pg210-216; Horner Arty pg 483;

### 5 November 1967: Security, Operation Santa Fe

*Significant* · 4 Fd Regt in contact

Australian: 1 killed.

Several enemy got through the defensive perimeter of 108 Field Battery and set up four Claymore mines aimed at its sleeping quarters. Two were fired, killing one gunner; the other two did not go off. Most of the battery was on duty at the time, or the casualties would have been far higher.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=697) · Report's sources: On the Offensive pg261; AWM95 7-7-20.pdf 7 RAR Jan 68 pg8; Intsum, 1/4/69.pdf p143;

### 1 December 1967: Security, Operation Forrest

*Typical: security* · 4 Fd Regt in contact

106 Field Battery saw two people moving in front of its position and fired Claymore mines. A search of the area found nothing.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=751) · Report's sources: Intsum, AWM95 1/4/74.pdf p97

### 2 December 1967: Ambush, Operation Forrest

*Significant* · Fire support for 2 RAR

Enemy: 4 killed, 1 wounded.

12 Platoon, D Company, 2 RAR, ambushed six Viet Cong, killing four and wounding one, and captured three weapons. A tracking team followed a blood trail until it was lost at a river, and 108 Field Battery fired on likely escape routes. Papers on one of the dead showed he was the commander of the Xuyen Moc district platoon.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=753) · Report's sources: Intsum, AWM95 1/4/74.pdf p99; AWM 569/R4/77; AWM95 7-2-47.pdf 2 RAR pg20-22; AWM95 1-4-74.pdf p5 SITREP;

### 10 December 1967: Security, Operation Forrest

*Significant* · 4 Fd Regt in contact

Australian: 1 killed.

106 Field Battery, supporting Operation Forrest from Fire Support Base Bravo, was hit by a single round from outside the perimeter, possibly a grenade or a mortar bomb. One Australian died of wounds, and an investigation began.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=768) · Report's sources: Intsum, AWM95 1/4/74.pdf p113; AWM95 1-4-74.pdf p20 SITREP;

### 2 January 1968: Bunker, Operation Forrest

*Significant* · Fire support for 7 RAR

Enemy: 2 killed.

12 Platoon, D Company, 7 RAR, found an enemy camp and met two enemy, who fled; sweeping the area, it met and killed another. 106 Field Battery probably fired in support.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=791) · Report's sources: Intsum Jan 68, AWM177 1/4/76; AWM95 7-7-19.pdf 7 RAR Jan 68 pg8-9; Intsum AWM95 1/4/80.pdf p107;

### 3 January 1968: Ambush, Operation Forrest

*Significant* · Fire support for 7 RAR

Enemy: 3 killed.

1 Platoon, A Company, 7 RAR, ambushed three enemy with two Claymore mines, machine-guns and rifles, killing two at once and the third, wounded, in the sweep that followed. Three SKS rifles were captured, and 106 Field Battery fired in support.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=794) · Report's sources: AWM95 7-7-19.pdf 7 RAR Jan 68 pg12; AWM95 7-7-23.pdf pg83 Contact Rept; Intsum AWM95 1/4/80.pdf p109;

### 27 February 1968: Arty/Mor, Operation Coburg

*Typical: artillery and mortar fire* · 4 Fd Regt in contact

C Company, 7 RAR, saw torches 800 metres from its position. 106 Field Battery and mortars fired on them, and the lights went out.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=1014) · Report's sources: AWM95 7-7-21.pdf 7 RAR Feb 68 pg102

### 1 March 1968: Contact

*Typical: artillery engagement* · 4 Fd Regt in contact

A patrol from 2 RAR saw 30 to 40 armed Viet Cong moving west in close formation along a track, carrying one wounded man on a stretcher. 106 and 108 Field Batteries fired on the area with unknown results.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=4781)

### 9 March 1968: Ambush, Operation Pinnaroo

*Significant* · Fire support for W Company, 2 RAR (New Zealand)

Enemy: 3 killed.

The perimeter defence of 2 Platoon, W Company, 2 RAR, ambushed five enemy, killing three and capturing one; the company caught the last man at dawn. Four weapons were captured, and 108 Field Battery and a US battery fired in support.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=1051) · Report's sources: AWM 7-3-58.pdf 3 RAR Mar 68, pg24-5; AWM95 7-2-50.pdf 2 RAR pg74; Intsum AWM95 1/4/91.pdf p93;

### 14 June 1970: Bunker, Operation Cung Chung

*Significant* · Fire support for 8 RAR

Australian: 1 killed, 3 wounded. Enemy: 1 killed.

A platoon of 8 RAR following a new track saw two enemy near a bunker and killed one; heavy fire from two bunkers then pinned the forward section. The enemy withdrew and reoccupied the bunkers, causing more casualties, which were evacuated under helicopter gunship cover while ammunition was flown in. After holding the enemy for an hour, the platoon pulled back 800 metres for artillery and to join tanks and carriers; the assault later that day met no resistance. One Australian was killed and three wounded.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=3944) · Report's sources: Intsum, AWM95 1/4/192.pdf p133; AWM 290 8/15 Cung Chung 1; AWM95 7-8-10.pdf 8 RAR Jun 70 pg77 Contact Rept;

### 18 February 1971: Bunker, Operation Phoi Hop

*Last* · Fire support for V Company, 2 RAR (New Zealand)

A bunker system found earlier was hit by artillery and air strikes and then assaulted and taken by 3 Platoon, V Company, 2 RAR. The enemy had left.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=4332) · Report's sources: AWM 290 2/R569/7/1 Pt1 2 RAR After Action Reports
