## Notable contacts

### 7 June 1966: Contact, Operation Hardihood

*First* · 1 ATF in contact

1 ATF's artillery fired on enemy mortar positions at Phuoc Long, a fortified Viet Cong village where the positions were well dug in and linked by tunnels. The Viet Cong had fired about 20 rounds.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=52) · Report's sources: 1ATF Intsum AWM95 1/4/3.pdf p141

### 4 February 1968: Contact, Operation Coburg

*Significant* · 1 ATF in contact

Enemy: 18 killed.

Australian and US artillery fired on an enemy mortar position. The next morning more than 18 bodies were found, nine of which were credited to 1 ATF's artillery.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=4705) · Report's sources: AWM95 1-4-85.pdf pg15 Sitrep; Intsum, AWM95 1/4/85.pdf p86;

### 26 May 1968: Security, Operation Toan Thang 1

*Significant* · Fire support for 1 RAR

Australian: 1 killed.

To draw attention from the attack on Balmoral, the enemy fired on Fire Support Base Coral for 30 minutes with about 60 mortar bombs and 20 recoilless-rifle rounds. One Australian was killed and two wounded; enemy casualties were not known.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=1324) · Report's sources: McAulay pg226; AWM95 7-1-78.pdf pt 1, 1 RAR May 68 pg22; Intsum, AWM95 1/4/100.pdf p119;

### 26 May 1968: Bunker, Operation Toan Thang 1

*Significant* · In 1 RAR's contact

Enemy: 17 killed.

Tanks of C Squadron, 1 Armoured Regiment, and D Company, 1 RAR, attacked a bunker system near Fire Support Base Coral that had been found the day before. The infantry protected the tanks while they cleared the jungle with canister rounds and blew up or crushed the bunkers; the infantry also used flamethrowers, and rocket-propelled grenades had no effect on the tanks. By dusk 14 to 18 bunkers had been destroyed without Australian loss, and at least seven enemy killed, with more buried in their bunkers.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=1325) · Report's sources: McAulay pg246-253; AWM95 7-1-78.pdf pt 1, 1 RAR May 68 pg22; AWM95 7-1-79.pdf 1 RAR Jun 68 pg75; AWM95 2-3-9.pdf pg40 Contact Report;

### 28 May 1968: Security, Operation Toan Thang 1

*Significant* · Fire support for 3 RAR

Australian: 1 killed, 8 wounded. Enemy: 55 killed, 10 wounded.

The North Vietnamese renewed their attack on Fire Support Base Balmoral with a heavy mortar and rocket bombardment and a ground attack by two battalions of the 165th Regiment. The attack broke up on the wire under artillery and helicopter gunship fire. One Australian was killed and six to eight wounded; about 47 to 55 enemy were killed and six captured.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=1330) · Report's sources: AWM95 2-3-9.pdf pg46 Contact Rept AFV; Scorpion Stings pg118 ; Intsum, AWM95 1/4/100.pdf p122;

### 10 January 1969: Bunker, Operation Goodwood

*Significant* · Fire support for 9 RAR

Australian: 1 killed, 6 wounded. Enemy: 10 killed.

3 Platoon, A Company, 9 RAR, sweeping an area, came under fire from a large bunker system with heavy and light machine-guns, rocket-propelled grenades and Claymores. The fight drew in 2 Platoon and lasted three and a half hours, until contact was broken at dusk with the help of artillery, mortars and helicopter gunships. One Australian was killed and six wounded; ten enemy were killed.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=1900) · Report's sources: Intsum, AWM95 1/4/136.pdf p92; AWM95 7-9-8.pdf 9 RAR Jan 69, Contact Rept pg32

*Typical: patrol* · 1 ATF in contact

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=3741) · Report's sources: Intsum, AWM95 1/4/186.pdf p65; AWM95 7-7-37.pdf 7 RAR pg108

### 31 March 1971: Bunker, Operation Briar Patch

*Significant* · Fire support for 2 RAR

Australian: 2 killed, 6 wounded. Enemy: 2 killed, 6 wounded.

1 Platoon, A Company, 2 RAR/NZ, following a freshly used track, fired on five enemy ten metres ahead, and was soon attacked on three sides with machine-guns, mortars, rockets, grenades and satchel charges by the headquarters and a company of the D445 Battalion. Gunships had trouble telling friend from foe, and a spotter aircraft, a medical helicopter and two gunships were hit by ground fire. After two hours, with ammunition low, the platoon fought its way out, abandoning its packs and the wounded's weapons, and 3 RAR's assault pioneers drove off the enemy's pursuit. Nine Australians were wounded, two of whom died, one of them an RAAF crewman. The enemy recovered a machine-gun, rifles, 30 Claymores and explosives from the battlefield.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=4445) · Report's sources: Intsum, AWM95 1/4/216.pdf p121; AWM279, item 723/R5/117. 3RAR Combat Operations After ActionReport, Op Briar Patch; AWM95, item 3/8/6; AWM95 7-2-86.pdf pg85-89; AWM95 7-3-74.pdf pg294 Contact Rept;

### 7 June 1971: Bunker, Operation Overlord

*Significant* · Fire support for 3 RAR

Australian: 3 killed, 8 wounded. Enemy: 5 killed.

5 Platoon, B Company, 3 RAR, found a bunker system held by the 3rd Battalion of the 33rd North Vietnamese Regiment, and after a night of artillery and mortar fire went in at dawn. It was met by rifle and heavy machine-gun fire from bunkers 15 metres away and pinned down for most of the day under repeated attacks by helicopter gunships and Cobras; the forward observer was killed, and a helicopter bringing ammunition was shot down, two of its crew dying. In the afternoon tanks of C Squadron, 1 Armoured Regiment, D Company and carriers of A Squadron, 3 Cavalry Regiment, attacked the system and secured it. Three Australians were killed, two of them RAAF, and twelve wounded. The company commander reported that a marker balloon worked where smoke could not rise through the high canopy, and that several machine-guns failed.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=4525) · Report's sources: Intsum, AWM95 1/4/225.pdf p95; AWM288, 569/1/25, Operation Overlord; AWM95 7-3-78.pdf pg284 Contact Rept;

### 12 June 1971: Patrol, Operation Overlord

*Significant* · Fire support for 3 Cav Regt

Australian: 8 killed, 2 wounded.

Three armoured personnel carriers of A Squadron, 3 Cavalry Regiment, carrying men of D and E Platoon, were patrolling a track when the leading driver stopped at an illumination canister he suspected was part of a mine. The enemy, of the 274th Regiment, opened fire, and a rocket-propelled grenade hit a pile of Claymore mines on the back of the last carrier, which exploded, killing its crew and five of D and E Platoon. Eight Australians were killed in all, and two wounded.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=4539) · Report's sources: Intsum AWM95 1-4-225.pdf pg122; AWM109 Ops 1ATF \[O\]; Scorpion Stings pg265;

### 23 October 1971: Transport

*Last* · 1 ATF in contact

A vehicle from Duc Thanh carrying three members of the Australian Army Training Team was fired on. There were no casualties and no damage.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=4668) · Report's sources: Intel V-dat Base; Contact Data Base
