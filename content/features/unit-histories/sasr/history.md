## Notable contacts

### 25 June 1966: Security, Operation TAOR

*First* · SASR in contact

The SAS squadron came under machine-gun and rifle fire on its left flank during the night. A standing patrol then saw four or five enemy in a paddy field 120 metres away, but they disappeared before they could be fired on.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=105) · Report's sources: Horner, SAS pg180; AWM95 7-12-1.pdf 3 SAS Jun 66 pg10; 20

### 12 December 1967: Ambush, Operation Forrest

*Significant* · SASR in contact

Enemy: 7 killed, 1 wounded.

A long-range patrol of 1 SAS Squadron ambushed seven Viet Cong, five men and two women, dressed in mixed clothing and carrying a large amount of food. All seven were killed and three weapons captured.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=774) · Report's sources: Intsum, AWM95 1/4/74.pdf p116; AWM 569/R4/77; Horner SAS pg230; AWM95 7-10-12.pdf 1 SAS pg124

### 18 March 1968: Ambush, Operation LRRP

*Significant* · SASR in contact

Enemy: 21 killed.

A 1 ATF long-range patrol of 2 SAS Squadron set a mine ambush of Claymores and explosives, which went off under a tractor and trailer carrying ammunition, weapons and equipment. Fifteen Viet Cong bodies were counted, and a defector from the unit later said 21 had died; a recoilless rifle was captured and the tractor destroyed. Helicopter gunships attacked the area, setting off secondary explosions, and the patrol was extracted.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=1090) · Report's sources: Horner SAS, pg8-15; AWM95 7-11-1.pdf 2 SAS pg156; Intsum AWM95 1/4/91.pdf p119;

### 17 April 1968: Ambush, Operation LRRP

*Typical: ambush* · SASR in contact

Enemy: 7 killed.

A 1 ATF long-range patrol of 2 SAS Squadron was in ambush when eight enemy came in two groups of four. Claymores wiped out the second group, and the patrol killed most of the first, six or seven in all. As it withdrew it was fired on by four more enemy with a machine-gun, and it was extracted.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=1164) · Report's sources: Intsum, AWM95 1/4/94 pt3.pdf p104; AWM95 7-11-2.pdf 2 SAS pg130

### 26 April 1968: Patrol, Operation LRRP

*Typical: patrol* · SASR in contact

Enemy: 4 killed.

A 19-man SAS patrol sent to ambush the recently abandoned Fire Support Base Dyke split up to reach a group of enemy it had seen. The main party then came on four more at seven metres and killed all four with rifles and grenade launchers, before attacking the other group.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=1183) · Report's sources: Horner SAS, pg254-5; AWM95 7-11-2.pdf 2 SAS pg160;

### 10 June 1968: Bunker, Operation LRRP

*Significant* · SASR in contact

Enemy: 8 killed.

The record gives no account of this SAS patrol's contact beyond the patrol commander's name; eight enemy were recorded killed.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=1357) · Report's sources: Horner SAS pg259; Intsum, AWM95 1/4/103.pdf p190; AWM95 7-11-4.pdf 2 SAS pg91

### 15 June 1968: Security, Operation LRRP

*Significant* · SASR in contact

Enemy: 6 killed, 2 wounded.

A 1 ATF long-range patrol of 2 SAS Squadron made contact with an estimated 15 enemy, killing six and possibly two more. There were no Australian casualties, and the patrol was extracted.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=1360) · Report's sources: Intsum, AWM95 1/4/103.pdf p195; Horner SAS pg 260; AWM95 7-11-4.pdf 2 SAS pg106;

### 5 July 1968: Ambush, Operation LRRP

*Significant* · SASR in contact

Enemy: 13 killed.

A six-man 1 ATF long-range patrol of 2 SAS Squadron engaged more than 25 enemy in khaki and green, armed with a machine-gun and rifles, moving north. Thirteen enemy were killed, and possibly more; there were no Australian casualties, though a grenade launcher was damaged.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=1385) · Report's sources: Intsum, AWM95 1/4/109.pdf p70; AWM95 7-11-5.pdf 2 SAS pg141; Horner SAS pg262;

### 5 October 1968: Ambush, Operation LRRP

*Significant* · SASR in contact

Enemy: 8 killed.

A 1 ATF long-range patrol of 2 SAS Squadron ambushed three ox-carts and eight enemy on a track, killing all eight. Fired on by six to eight more enemy, it called in helicopter gunships, saw six more ox-carts on the track, and was extracted.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=1605) · Report's sources: Intsum, AWM95 1/4/121.pdf p82; Horner SAS pg274; AWM95 7-11-8.pdf 2 SAS pg93

### 23 October 1968: Ambush, Operation LRRP

*Significant* · SASR in contact

Australian: 1 wounded. Enemy: 7 killed.

A 1 ATF long-range patrol of 2 SAS Squadron made contact with seven enemy, killing all seven and capturing four rifles. One Australian was wounded.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=1657) · Report's sources: Intsum, AWM95 1/4/121.pdf p125; AWM95 7-11-8.pdf 2 SAS pg122; Horner SAS pg275; AWM103 447A 2 SAS Contact Repts

### 5 May 1970: Bunker, Operation LRRP

*Typical: bunker* · SASR in contact

Enemy: 2 killed.

An SAS patrol found a bunker and a hut and moved into the camp, where five enemy sat round a cooking fire. They saw the patrol and tried to get away; the patrol fired rifles and grenades, and a sweep found two bodies and a rifle. The patrol leader remarked that moving eleven men through the jungle was hard, especially with Vietnamese troops.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=3822) · Report's sources: AWM103 R723/1/1/3 SAS Contact Repts

### 6 June 1970: Security, Operation LRRP

*Typical: security* · SASR in contact

Enemy: 3 killed.

An SAS patrol of 1 SAS Squadron engaged three enemy who were following it, killing two and possibly the third. More enemy movement was heard, and old camps were found nearby.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=3926) · Report's sources: Horner SAS pg354; AWM95 7-10-19.pdf 1 SAS pg128; AWM103 R723/1/1/3 SAS Contact Repts

### 26 August 1971: Patrol, Operation LRRP

*Last* · SASR in contact

Enemy: 1 killed.

An SAS patrol had stopped to make radio contact when its second in command heard movement by a creek and saw three Viet Cong following the patrol's tracks. He fired at five to seven metres, killing one, probably a platoon leader of an enemy rear-services unit; the others returned fire, and the patrol suppressed the area and moved forward. A rifle, a pack and documents were captured.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=4635) · Report's sources: AWM95 7-11-19.pdf 2 SAS pg142; AWM103 R723/1/1/3 SAS Contact Rept; Intsum, AWM95 1/4/232.pdf p211
