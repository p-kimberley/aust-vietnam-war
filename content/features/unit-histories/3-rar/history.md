### 19 December 1967: Patrol, Operation TAOR

*First* · 3 RAR in contact

The advance party of 3 RAR on patrol saw two Viet Cong in black, one carrying two weapons and the other a sack, 100 to 150 metres away. They fled, and a tracking team lost their trail. There were no casualties.

### 1 February 1968: Security, Operation Coburg

*Significant* · 3 RAR in contact

Australian: 1 killed, 19 wounded. Enemy: 42 killed, 5 wounded.

During the Tet Offensive a Viet Cong battalion seized much of Ba Ria, a town of 15,000, and the province headquarters asked 1 ATF for help. A Company, 3 RAR, less a platoon, with nine armoured personnel carriers of 3 Troop, A Squadron, 3 Cavalry Regiment, was rushed in by carrier to protect the US compound and other key buildings, and fought through a town full of uncleared houses, infiltrators and rocket teams. One Australian was killed and nineteen wounded; 42 enemy were recorded killed.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=903) · Report's sources: R Hall and A T Ross,

### 18 February 1968: Security, Operation Coburg

*Significant* · 3 RAR in contact

Australian: 8 killed, 27 wounded. Enemy: 4 killed.

Fire Support Base Anderson was attacked at night by at least a company and a mortar platoon. After an accurate bombardment of mortar bombs and rockets, the enemy assaulted in waves through the rear of a US battery and 3 RAR's mortar lines, breaking into the perimeter; the machine-guns of 1 Troop, A Squadron, 3 Cavalry Regiment's carriers, the US battery and mortar fire drove them back, and further probes failed at the wire. Four enemy bodies were found, with signs of many more casualties. Seven Australians were killed and about 24 wounded, and one American killed; four sappers of 1 Field Squadron died when a mortar bomb hit their standing patrol.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=1005) · Report's sources: Intsum, AWM95 1/4/85.pdf p115; When the Scorpion Stings pg 96; AWM95 7-3-55.pdf 3 RAR Feb 68 pg116, 279,285; See FSPB GR AWM95 7-3-61.pdf pg258; Official History pg303. AWM95 1-4-85.pdf pg44 Sitrep;

### 13 May 1968: Security, Operation Toan Thang 1

*Significant* · In 1 RAR's contact

Australian: 9 killed, 28 wounded. Enemy: 55 killed.

Fire Support Base Coral, north-east of Saigon, was attacked by an estimated battalion with very heavy and accurate mortar fire, rockets and a ground assault. The enemy overran a 105 mm gun position and 1 RAR's mortar platoon, and were only driven out by dawn, with the support of a gunship aircraft and helicopter gunships; 1 RAR's rifle companies were out in ambush positions one or two kilometres away. Nine Australians were killed and 28 wounded; 55 North Vietnamese were counted dead and one captured.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=1233) · Report's sources: Intsum, AWM95 1/4/100.pdf p93; Lex McAulay; On the Offensive pg358

### 26 May 1968: Security, Operation Toan Thang 1

*Significant* · 3 RAR in contact

Australian: 3 killed, 16 wounded. Enemy: 6 killed, 30 wounded.

The 165th North Vietnamese Regiment attacked Fire Support Base Balmoral, held by 3 RAR with tanks and carriers: heavy mortar fire, followed by a ground attack by about a company, supported by rockets and machine-guns, from an estimated battalion group. Three Australians were killed and sixteen wounded; six enemy were recorded killed.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=1323) · Report's sources: McAulay pg237-8; Scorpion Stings pg 114-116; On the Offensive pg 384; AWM95 2-3-9.pdf pg36 Contact Rept AFV;

### 28 May 1968: Security, Operation Toan Thang 1

*Significant* · 3 RAR in contact

Australian: 1 killed, 8 wounded. Enemy: 55 killed, 10 wounded.

The North Vietnamese renewed their attack on Fire Support Base Balmoral with a heavy mortar and rocket bombardment and a ground attack by two battalions of the 165th Regiment. The attack broke up on the wire under artillery and helicopter gunship fire. One Australian was killed and six to eight wounded; about 47 to 55 enemy were killed and six captured.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=1330) · Report's sources: AWM95 2-3-9.pdf pg46 Contact Rept AFV; Scorpion Stings pg118 ; Intsum, AWM95 1/4/100.pdf p122;

### 8 August 1968: Patrol, Operation Long Dien

*Significant* · 3 RAR in contact

Australian: 3 killed, 4 wounded. Enemy: 9 killed, 3 wounded.

5 Platoon, B Company, 3 RAR, was covering 4 Platoon's search for a missing South Vietnamese battalion commander at Long Dien when its forward sections came under heavy fire from a Viet Cong platoon. Three Australians were killed and four wounded, and nine Viet Cong were killed. The enemy took a radio from an Australian signaller, and may have taken weapons from the casualties.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=977) · Report's sources: Intel V-dat Base; AWM95 7-3-55.pdf 3 RAR Feb 68 pg273 Contact Rept;

### 22 September 1968: Patrol, Operation Nowra

*Typical: patrol* · 3 RAR in contact

Australian: 4 wounded. Enemy: 6 killed.

9 Platoon, C Company, 3 RAR, coming down a hill, met about 20 enemy coming up. The enemy fired rifles and rocket-propelled grenades into the trees, causing splinter wounds; a dog handler and the forward scout were evacuated. Six enemy were possibly killed.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=1569) · Report's sources: Intsum, AWM95 1/4/116.pdf 182; AWM95 7-3-65.pdf 3 RAR Sep 68 pg85-7

### 10 October 1968: Ambush, Operation Windsor

*Typical: ambush* · 3 RAR in contact

Enemy: 2 killed, 11 wounded.

A platoon of B Company, 3 RAR, had covered 200 metres of the Firestone Trail with six Claymores when more than 80 enemy began filing past. The three-man killing party fired the Claymores, one of which failed, and withdrew to the rest of the platoon while artillery was brought down around the killing ground. A sweep the next morning found two bodies and eleven drag marks.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=1624) · Report's sources: Intsum, AWM95 1/4/121.pdf p93; AWM95 7-3-66.pdf 3 RAR Oct 68 pg226 Contact Rept;

### 19 October 1968: Ambush, Operation Capital

*Significant* · 3 RAR in contact

Australian: 2 killed, 6 wounded. Enemy: 7 killed, 7 wounded.

2 Platoon, A Company, 3 RAR, in ambush near its company base, shot an enemy soldier, and ten minutes later was attacked by at least a platoon with rocket-propelled grenades and machine-guns that began to work round its flank. A rocket hit a machine-gun team, and two Australians died of wounds; six were wounded. 1 Platoon covered 2 Platoon's withdrawal under artillery and very accurate helicopter gunship fire, and after further contacts around the company base the company broke contact. Seven enemy were killed and up to nine more possibly killed.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=1652) · Report's sources: AWM95 7-3-66.pdf 3 RAR Oct 68 pg14, 261 Contact Rept; Intsum, AWM95 1/4/121.pdf p118

### 2 March 1971: Security, Operation Phoi Hop

*Typical: security* · 3 RAR in contact

Australian: 2 killed, 2 wounded. Enemy: 3 wounded.

D Company, 3 RAR, was in a night harbour on the edge of a paddy field when 11 Platoon saw movement and opened fire with Claymores and machine-guns, then fired on about ten enemy trying to recover bodies. Enemy right on 10 Platoon's perimeter answered with heavy rifle fire and threw a satchel charge inside it, killing the platoon commander; another soldier was shot dead, and two were wounded. The enemy, signalling with tin whistles, attacked D and C Companies through the night, and helicopter gunships, artillery illumination and a tank's searchlight were used against them until they withdrew.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=4349) · Report's sources: AWM109 Ops 1ATF \[P\]; Intsum AWM98 320 pt 2; AWM95 7-3-72.pdf pg25 Contact Rept; Sitrep, AWM95 1/4/216.pdf p9

### 20 March 1971: Bunker, Operation Briar Patch

*Typical: bunker* · 3 RAR in contact

Australian: 2 killed, 2 wounded.

Half of 8 Platoon, C Company, 3 RAR, heard chopping, digging and a dog barking near a track, fired grenades at the sound, and swept forward into a bunker system. The platoon commander and a machine-gunner were wounded and the patrol pinned down by heavy fire, and it pulled back; the commander later died of wounds. With their smoke grenades used up, the patrol could not mark its position, so the helicopter gunships could not fire, and one gunship was hit and its co-pilot killed. The rest of the company, the assault pioneers in carriers and a troop of tanks converged, and the enemy, probably a stay-behind group, withdrew. The platoon commander was criticised for attacking without a reconnaissance.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=4406) · Report's sources: Sitrep, AWM95 1/4/216.pdf p47; AWM279, 723/R5/117, 3RAR Combat Operations After Action Report, Op Briar Patch; AWM95 7-3-74.pdf pg291 Contact Rept;

### 7 June 1971: Bunker, Operation Overlord

*Significant* · 3 RAR in contact

Australian: 3 killed, 8 wounded. Enemy: 5 killed.

5 Platoon, B Company, 3 RAR, found a bunker system held by the 3rd Battalion of the 33rd North Vietnamese Regiment, and after a night of artillery and mortar fire went in at dawn. It was met by rifle and heavy machine-gun fire from bunkers 15 metres away and pinned down for most of the day under repeated attacks by helicopter gunships and Cobras; the forward observer was killed, and a helicopter bringing ammunition was shot down, two of its crew dying. In the afternoon tanks of C Squadron, 1 Armoured Regiment, D Company and carriers of A Squadron, 3 Cavalry Regiment, attacked the system and secured it. Three Australians were killed, two of them RAAF, and twelve wounded. The company commander reported that a marker balloon worked where smoke could not rise through the high canopy, and that several machine-guns failed.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=4525) · Report's sources: Intsum, AWM95 1/4/225.pdf p95; AWM288, 569/1/25, Operation Overlord; AWM95 7-3-78.pdf pg284 Contact Rept;

### 1 September 1971: Contact, Operation Cudlee Creek

*Last* · 3 RAR in contact

7 Platoon, C Company, 3 RAR, made contact with two enemy, who withdrew. The sweep found their tracks, a sandal and a hat.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=4638) · Report's sources: Intsum, AWM95 1/4/235.pdf p68
