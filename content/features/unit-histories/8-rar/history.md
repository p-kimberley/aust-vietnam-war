## Notable contacts

### 11 November 1969: Patrol

*First* · 8 RAR in contact

A patrol from 8 RAR was fired on with a single shot by an enemy soldier in green moving east, who withdrew towards Hoa Long. There were no Australian casualties, and enemy casualties were not known.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=3121) · Report's sources: Intel V-dat Base

### 21 December 1969: Bunker, Operation Atherton

*Typical: bunker* · 8 RAR in contact

Australian: 1 killed, 2 wounded. Enemy: 1 killed, 3 wounded.

10 Platoon, D Company, 8 RAR, came on 30 to 40 well-trained enemy in six bunkers, and an engineer was shot. With 11 Platoon's support the platoon assaulted, silenced one bunker and beat off a counter-attack in nearly three hours of fighting, with helicopter gunships and artillery in support; one Australian was shot dead and two wounded. Dusk ended the fight, and when the company returned with tanks the next day the enemy had gone, leaving one body.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=3278) · Report's sources: Sitrep, AWM95 1/4/176.pdf p77; AWM109 Ops 1ATF \[A\]; AWM95 7-8-3.pdf 8 RAR Dec 69 pg130-134; AWM95 7-8-4.pdf 8 RAR Dec 69 pg150 Contact Rept;

### 13 January 1970: Ambush, Operation TAOR

*Typical: ambush* · 8 RAR in contact

Enemy: 8 killed, 2 wounded.

A patrol of 10 Platoon, D Company, 8 RAR, was in ambush at a track junction near the Suoi Hoa Nuoc when the machine-gun sentry heard a radio playing and waited until it was in the killing area. Claymores, a machine-gun and grenade launchers were fired, and the killing ground was then searched with grenades in a set pattern. Eight enemy, one of them a woman, were killed and two wounded Viet Cong captured, with weapons and grenades. The patrol commander stressed keeping fire low, with interlocking arcs.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=3363) · Report's sources: Sitrep, AWM95 1/4/179.pdf p41; AWM95 7-8-5.pdf 8 RAR Jan 70 pg118 Contact Rept;

### 15 February 1970: Ambush, Operation Hammersley

*Significant* · 8 RAR in contact

Australian: 3 wounded. Enemy: 10 killed, 22 wounded.

9 Platoon, C Company, 8 RAR, was carried by tanks to within a kilometre of a carefully chosen ambush site and slipped in unseen while the tanks made a diversion, with engineers checking the site for mines and 15 Claymores laid along the track. It let the first of four enemy groups pass, and wiped out the second and third with its Claymores; the first and fourth counter-attacked. Running low on ammunition, the platoon was supported by mortars, artillery and helicopter gunships, and within 40 minutes the tanks of 1 Armoured Regiment came back, cleared the killing ground and stayed through the night. Three Australians were wounded; ten enemy were counted dead, and 22 drag marks were found.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=3516) · Report's sources: Scorpion Stings pg205; Intsum, AWM95 1/4/181.pdf Pt2 p172; AWM95 7-8-7.pdf 8 RAR Feb 70 pg87 Contact Rept; Sitrep, AWM95 1/4/181.pdf Pt2 p51

### 18 February 1970: Bunker, Operation Hammersley

*Significant* · 8 RAR in contact

Australian: 2 killed, 13 wounded. Enemy: 12 killed, 10 wounded.

C Company, 8 RAR, captured a Viet Cong who said 40 enemy were in a nearby bunker system protected by mines. The company attacked with carriers of B Squadron, 3 Cavalry Regiment, and tanks of 1 Armoured Regiment; a carrier was destroyed by two rocket-propelled grenades, killing two Australians and wounding more. After air and artillery strikes a second assault led by the tanks went through the bunkers to a deep creek, but the system was also protected by an anti-tank minefield, and the force was ordered to withdraw, taking more casualties from enemy who had reoccupied the bunkers. Twelve enemy were killed, mostly by the tanks' canister and high-explosive rounds; one tank was hit eight times and kept fighting. The system was later hit by B-52 bombers.

### 18 February 1970: Patrol, Operation Hammersley

*Typical: patrol* · 8 RAR in contact

Australian: 2 wounded. Enemy: 3 killed.

A section of B Squadron, 3 Cavalry Regiment's carriers, on a jungle trail to an ambush position, was ambushed by a rocket team that hit the leading carrier twice, piercing the hull and wounding its crew. The carriers' fire drove the enemy off, and 5 Platoon, B Company, 8 RAR, swept the area and secured a landing zone for the wounded. Three Australians were wounded. The enemy had come from a bunker system not then known; three bodies found a week later were credited to this contact.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=3538) · Report's sources: Scorpion Stings pg209; Sitrep, AWM95 1/4/181.pdf Pt2 p57; AWM95 7-8-7.pdf 8 RAR Feb 70 pg103 Contact Rept;

### 18 February 1970: Bunker, Operation Hammersley

*Significant* · 8 RAR in contact

Australian: 8 wounded. Enemy: 6 killed.

After C Company, 8 RAR's heavy fight that day, B Company was rushed by armoured personnel carrier into blocking positions. 4 Platoon had just dismounted when a section came under heavy rifle fire and rocket-propelled grenades; one carrier was holed and a tank hit. Sweeping forward, the platoon saw Claymore wires ten metres into the jungle and was pulled back. Eight Australians were wounded, all by rockets; six enemy were killed, three of whose bodies were found a week later.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=3544) · Report's sources: AWM98, item R723-1-219; Headquarters, Australian Forces Vietnam, Reports General Investigation into Accidental Shooting 8RAR \[Royal Australian Regiment\], Long hais - 18 Feb 70. ### 25 February 1970: Security, Operation Hammersley

*Typical: security* · 8 RAR in contact

Australian: 1 killed, 2 wounded.

A Company, 8 RAR, set off what was probably an M16 mine in the south-east of the Long Hai Hills, killing one Australian and wounding two.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=3576) · Report's sources: Sitrep, AWM95 1/4/181 Pt2.pdf p80; AWM95 7-8-7.pdf 8 RAR Feb 70 pg52

### 28 February 1970: Patrol, Operation Hammersley

*Significant* · 8 RAR in contact

Australian: 7 killed, 13 wounded.

1 Platoon, A Company, 8 RAR, was moving carefully to an ambush site in the heavily mined south-east of the Long Hai Hills, with engineers clearing the way. It had reached the site, and was gathered around platoon headquarters, when one of the engineers set off an M16 'jumping jack' mine. Seven or eight Australians were killed and thirteen wounded.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=3584) · Report's sources: Sitrep, AWM95 1/4/181 Pt2.pdf p90; Combat Battalion p154; AWM95 7-8-7.pdf pg70;

### 6 March 1970: Contact, Operation Hamilton

*Significant* · 8 RAR in contact

Australian: 2 killed, 6 wounded.

B Company, 8 RAR, found an old, rusty M16 mine in the jungle six kilometres south of Xuyen Moc. While it was being destroyed the platoon deployed into all-round defence to look at some old bunkers, and someone set off another. Two Australians were killed and six wounded.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=3609) · Report's sources: AWM Records Ops 1ATF \[E\]; Sitrep, AWM95 1/4/183 Pt2.pdf p20; AWM95 2-1-6.pdf pg57 Sitrep 6/3/70; AWM95 7-8-7.pdf pg132

### 20 April 1970: Transport, Operation Concrete

*Significant* · In 3 Cav Regt's contact

Australian: 1 killed, 10 wounded.

An armoured personnel carrier of 3 Troop, B Squadron, 3 Cavalry Regiment, carrying troops of 5 Platoon, B Company, 8 RAR, on a track off Route 23, hit a 40-pound anti-tank mine. The carrier was badly damaged; one crewman was killed and about ten of those aboard wounded, one of them an engineer.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=3763) · Report's sources: AWM109 Ops 1ATF \[C\]; Intsum, AWM95 1/4/186.pdf p87; AWM95 7-8-9.pdf 8 RAR Apr 70 pg 23;

### 3 May 1970: Ambush, Operation Nudgee

*Significant* · 8 RAR in contact

Australian: 6 wounded. Enemy: 2 killed, 5 wounded.

3 Platoon, A Company, 8 RAR, on its way to an ambush position, met 40 to 50 Viet Cong armed with rifles, rockets, machine-guns and grenade launchers. The platoon formed a firm base, and the enemy attacked from both flanks, closing to within ten metres to recover their dead and wounded. After an hour 3 Troop, B Squadron, 3 Cavalry Regiment, arrived, and ten minutes later the enemy broke contact. Six Australians were wounded by rockets and grenades; two enemy bodies were found, and intelligence later put the enemy dead at over 50.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=3815) · Report's sources: Intsum, AWM95 1/4/189.pdf p105; Scorpion Stings pg222; AWM 723/R5/103; Combat Bn pg134; AWM95 7/8 Jun 70; AWM95 7-8-9.pdf 8 RAR Apr 70 pg138 Contact Rept;

### 12 August 1970: Ambush, Operation Cung Chung 2

*Significant* · 8 RAR in contact

Australian: 1 wounded. Enemy: 19 killed, 6 wounded.

8 Platoon, C Company, 8 RAR, ambushing Route 2 near Hoa Long, watched 50 to 60 Viet Cong move carefully into the village and moved its ambush to catch them on their way out. They came back at about 3 am, less alert, carrying packs, and headed straight for one group, so the ambush was sprung early; Claymores and four machine-guns in crossfire killed or wounded the whole first group, and the second withdrew with the wounded. 1 Troop, B Squadron, 3 Cavalry Regiment, came up quickly, drove off a counter-attack and ran down more of the enemy hiding in scrub. Nineteen Viet Cong were killed and six captured, with a large haul of weapons, food and supplies; one Australian was wounded.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=4092) · Report's sources: Intsum, AWM95 1/4/197.pdf p203; Scorpion Stings pg235;

### 9 October 1970: Ambush, Operation Cung Chung 3

*Last* · 8 RAR in contact

Enemy: 4 killed, 1 wounded.

9 Platoon, C Company, 8 RAR, ambushed six Viet Cong moving along Route 2. The ambush was sprung with Claymores, three of which failed, and machine-guns, and kept up until illumination from a spotter aircraft and carrier support arrived. Four enemy were killed and one North Vietnamese soldier was wounded and captured.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=4169) · Report's sources: Intsum, AWM95 1/4/201.pdf p190
