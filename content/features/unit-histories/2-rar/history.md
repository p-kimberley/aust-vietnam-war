## Notable contacts

### 11 June 1967: Security, Operation Darwin

*First* · 2 RAR in contact

A sentry of B Company, 2 RAR, saw an enemy soldier at the perimeter wire and threw a grenade. There were no enemy casualties.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=541) · Report's sources: AWM95 7-2-40.pdf 2 RAR Jun 67 pg75

### 23 October 1967: Ambush, Operation TAOR

*Typical: ambush* · 2 RAR in contact

Enemy: 4 killed, 6 wounded.

2 Platoon, A Company, 2 RAR, was in ambush on a track about a kilometre north of Long Tan, well inside the prohibited zone, when 16 Vietnamese came along it. The machine-gunner took a raised carrying pole for a weapon and opened fire, and the rest of the ambush joined in; the commander stopped the firing when he heard a woman's voice. They were all unarmed civilians: four were killed, six wounded and captured, and three more taken prisoner.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=672) · Report's sources: On the Offensive pg260. AWM95 7-2-45.pdf pt 1, 2 RAR pg83 Contact Rept;

### 30 November 1967: Patrol, Operation Forrest

*Significant* · 2 RAR in contact

Australian: 2 killed, 8 wounded.

A 12-man patrol of 2 Platoon, A Company, 2 RAR, was hit by a command-detonated mine, and the enemy opened fire. The patrol returned fire and called in artillery. Two Australians were killed and eight wounded.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=746) · Report's sources: Intsum, 1/4/69.pdf p191; AWM279 569/R4/77; On the Offensive pg264; AWM95 7-2-46.pdf 2 RAR pg99-101

### 26 January 1968: Bunker, Operation Coburg

*Significant* · 2 RAR in contact

Australian: 2 killed, 1 wounded. Enemy: 2 killed, 6 wounded.

5 Platoon, B Company, 2 RAR, ran into 15 to 20 enemy in a camp and called in artillery and mortars in a heavy fight. One Australian was killed, one died of wounds and one was wounded; the enemy left two bodies, blood trails and drag marks.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=847) · Report's sources: Intsum AWM95 1/4/80.pdf p157; On the Offensive pg295; ; AWM95 7-2-48.pdf 2 RAR pg75

### 26 January 1968: Bunker, Operation Coburg

*Significant* · 2 RAR in contact

Australian: 2 killed. Enemy: 2 killed, 4 wounded.

9 Platoon, C Company, 2 RAR, assaulted an enemy camp and had only just occupied its horseshoe-shaped defences when 20 or more enemy of the 274th Regiment and the Trang Bom district unit counter-attacked. The fight went on for three hours, the enemy attacking seven times with heavy machine-guns, and helicopter gunships, air strikes and artillery were called in. Two Australians were killed; two enemy were killed and more wounded, and the camp yielded weapons, packs and a large quantity of rice and food.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=852) · Report's sources: On the Offensive pg295; Intsum AWM95 1/4/80.pdf p157; AWM95 7-2-48.pdf 2 RAR pg77

### 27 January 1968: Bunker, Operation Coburg

*Typical: bunker* · 2 RAR in contact

Australian: 2 wounded. Enemy: 1 killed, 4 wounded.

Following up its bunker contact of the day before, C Company, 2 RAR, was fired on from a bunker, which it found and grenaded along with at least one more. It then came under fire from three sides and from snipers, with one platoon pinned down so that every movement drew fire, and two men were wounded. Artillery was fired, the wounded were evacuated, and the company was able to disengage; V Company, sent to help, arrived later over hard going. One enemy body and four blood trails were found, and two rifles and 700 pounds of food captured.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=856) · Report's sources: AWM95 7-2-48.pdf 2 RAR pg82; Intsum, AWM95 1/4/80.pdf p159;

### 31 January 1968: Bunker, Operation Coburg

*Significant* · 2 RAR in contact

Australian: 5 wounded. Enemy: 4 killed, 2 wounded.

A platoon made contact at 15 metres on the edge of an enemy camp, taking three casualties, and called in artillery and mortars. B Company, 2 RAR, assaulted the camp that afternoon but was pinned down by a machine-gun and rocket-propelled grenades, with enemy appearing from one bunker as soon as another was cleared. V Company, gunships and an air strike were sent to help, and after the wounded were evacuated B Company pulled back 300 metres from a bunker system much larger than first thought. Five Australians were wounded and four enemy killed.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=895) · Report's sources: AWM95 7-2-48.pdf 2 RAR pg100-103; AWM95 7-2-49.pdf 2 RAR pg18; Intsum, AWM95 1/4/80.pdf p169;

### 1 February 1968: Patrol, Operation Coburg

*Significant* · 2 RAR in contact

Enemy: 11 killed.

The battle for Trang Bom, during the Tet Offensive. D Company, 2 RAR, carried in armoured personnel carriers of A Squadron, 3 Cavalry Regiment, was sent with South Vietnamese forces to drive a Viet Cong force out of the village. Heavy rocket and automatic fire kept them out at first; helicopter gunships attacked, and the advance was slowed by the need to move civilians still in their homes to the market place. The enemy withdrew under cover of smoke from burning houses, came back that night, and was driven out again the next day with carrier and artillery support. Across the two days 19 enemy were recorded killed, most by artillery and carriers; no Australians were lost, but six civilians were killed by artillery.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=906) · Report's sources: R Hall and A T Ross,

### 2 March 1968: Patrol, Operation Pinnaroo

*Significant* · 2 RAR in contact

Australian: 1 killed, 13 wounded.

B Company, 2 RAR, took the Dinh Co hill in the north-west of the Long Hai Hills. An engineer set off a booby trap in the temple, killing himself and wounding 13 others. The company commander reported the area thick with mines and booby traps, and that the enemy were placing them at places Westerners were likely to visit.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=1033) · Report's sources: On the Offensive pg334; AWM95 7-2-50.pdf 2 RAR pg23,25; Intsum AWM95 1/4/91.pdf p78;

### 2 August 1970: Security, Operation Nathan

*Typical: security* · 2 RAR in contact

Australian: 2 killed, 3 wounded.

As 2 Platoon relieved 1 Platoon, A Company, 2 RAR, in a bunker system taken the day before, the commanding officer was showing his South Vietnamese counterpart round with a protection party when a booby-trapped 155 mm shell went off. Two Australians were killed and three wounded; both commanding officers were among the wounded and lost legs.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=4081) · Report's sources: AWM95 7-2-61.pdf pg137; Church

### 8 August 1970: Patrol, Operation Cung Chung 2

*Typical: patrol* · 2 RAR in contact

Australian: 8 wounded.

2 RAR's Anti-Tank Platoon set off a Chinese mine while examining an enemy camp. Seven Australians, a New Zealander and a South Vietnamese soldier were wounded.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=4087) · Report's sources: Intsum, AWM95 1/4/197.pdf p190; J M Church

### 15 December 1970: Ambush, Operation Cung Chung 3

*Significant* · 2 RAR in contact

Australian: 2 wounded. Enemy: 10 killed, 1 wounded.

6 Platoon, B Company, 2 RAR/NZ, was in ambush in a rubber plantation covering the house of a known Viet Cong food supplier. Ten unarmed people who saw the ambush and ran were not fired on. Later, after torches were flashed at the house and a lantern put out, the platoon heard people creeping towards it for 40 minutes, and sprang the ambush when there was whispering right in front of the machine-gun. The enemy threw a grenade and fought on from the flank until armoured personnel carriers were heard coming. Two Australians were wounded; ten Viet Cong were killed, and a prisoner later said fourteen had died.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=4245) · Report's sources: Intsum, AWM95 1/4/206.pdf p222; AWM95 7-2-73.pdf pg86 Contact Rept;

### 31 March 1971: Bunker, Operation Briar Patch

*Significant* · 2 RAR in contact

Australian: 2 killed, 6 wounded. Enemy: 2 killed, 6 wounded.

1 Platoon, A Company, 2 RAR/NZ, following a freshly used track, fired on five enemy ten metres ahead, and was soon attacked on three sides with machine-guns, mortars, rockets, grenades and satchel charges by the headquarters and a company of the D445 Battalion. Gunships had trouble telling friend from foe, and a spotter aircraft, a medical helicopter and two gunships were hit by ground fire. After two hours, with ammunition low, the platoon fought its way out, abandoning its packs and the wounded's weapons, and 3 RAR's assault pioneers drove off the enemy's pursuit. Nine Australians were wounded, two of whom died, one of them an RAAF crewman. The enemy recovered a machine-gun, rifles, 30 Claymores and explosives from the battlefield.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=4445) · Report's sources: Intsum, AWM95 1/4/216.pdf p121; AWM279, item 723/R5/117. 3RAR Combat Operations After ActionReport, Op Briar Patch; AWM95, item 3/8/6; AWM95 7-2-86.pdf pg85-89; AWM95 7-3-74.pdf pg294 Contact Rept;

### 29 April 1971: Transport

*Last* · 2 RAR in contact

A 2 RAR/NZ vehicle travelling on Route 2 was fired on with two rounds from the west.
