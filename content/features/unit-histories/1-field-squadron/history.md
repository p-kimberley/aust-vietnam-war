## Notable contacts

Each report is summarised in plain English by an AI model from the original, which is full of abbreviations and soldiers' shorthand; the original is on the Battle Map.

### 23 October 1965: Deployment, Operation 13/65

*First* · Engineer support for 1 RAR

Operation 13/65, 1 RAR's search-and-destroy operation north-east of the US 173rd Airborne Brigade's base at Bien Hoa, with a troop of armoured personnel carriers, 3 Field Troop, and 105 Field Battery and New Zealand's 161 Battery in direct support.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=10185) · Report's sources: AWM95, item 7/1/35, 1 Battalion Royal Australian Regiment, Narrative Annexes, 1-31 Oct 65, p. 18-19. McNeill, To Long Tan, p. 441.

### 10 January 1966: Installation, Operation 1/66

*Significant* · Engineer support for 1 RAR

Australian: 1 killed.

Engineers of 3 Field Troop pumped smoke and CS gas into tunnels found by B Company, 1 RAR, to reveal other entrances and deny them to the enemy. An engineer corporal inspecting a shaft was overcome by the gas and lack of air, and was dead when rescuers reached him; two of those who tried to reach him were evacuated. Documents, food, medical stores and a booby-trapped trapdoor were found in the tunnels and caches.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=10079) · Report's sources: Major Ian McFarlane's Operational Analysis is at AWM95, item 7/1/56, 1 Battalion, Royal Australian Regiment, Annexes, 1-31 Jan 1966, p. 12-19.

### 17 March 1966: Mine/Booby trap, Operation Silver City

*Significant* · Engineer support for 1 RAR

Australian: 4 wounded.

A Claymore mine went off in front of a small transport vehicle carrying rice out of a captured cache for B Company, 1 RAR, wounding two sappers of 3 Field Troop and an American soldier.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=10144) · Report's sources: AWM95, item 7/1/61, 1 Battalion, Royal Australian Regiment, Narrative, Annexes, 1-31 Mar 66, p. 22.

### 12 June 1966: Patrol, Operation Hardihood

*Significant* · Engineer support for 5 RAR

Australian: 1 killed, 3 wounded.

D Company, 5 RAR, was sweeping the north-east of Hoa Long for enemy mortar positions when its force came under light mortar fire. One Australian was killed and three wounded, from D and E Platoon, 1 APC Squadron and 1 Field Squadron. Radar placed the mortars at Long Phuoc village, and artillery fired on them with unknown results.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=66) · Report's sources: Cmdr

### 9 May 1967: Security, Operation Leeton

*Significant* · 1 Fd Sqn in contact

Australian: 2 killed, 4 wounded.

A sapper of 1 Field Squadron set off an M16 mine while laying it in the barrier minefield. Six were wounded, two of whom later died.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=4710) · Report's sources: AWM95 1-4-39.pdf pg78 Sitrep;

### 20 May 1967: Contact, Operation Leeton

*Significant* · 1 Fd Sqn in contact

Australian: 2 killed.

Two engineers of 1 Field Squadron were killed when an anti-lift device went off while they were laying M16 mines in the barrier minefield.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=4679) · Report's sources: AWM95 1-4-39.pdf p95 Sitrep

### 30 May 1967: Security, Operation Leeton

*Significant* · 1 Fd Sqn in contact

Australian: 1 killed, 2 wounded.

While laying the barrier minefield, a member of a mine-arming party from 7 RAR's assault pioneers set off an M16 mine, killing one sapper of 1 Field Squadron and wounding two.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=526) · Report's sources: On the Offensive pg 171; AWM95 7-7-2.pdf pg155

### 16 December 1967: Security

*Typical: security* · 1 Fd Sqn in contact

Australian: 2 wounded.

1 Field Squadron was helping a South Vietnamese battalion repair the minefield between Dat Do and Phuoc Hai when a South Vietnamese soldier stepped on an M16 mine. He was killed; two Australians and four South Vietnamese were wounded.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=4694) · Report's sources: AWM95 1-4-74.pdf p32 SITREP;

### 1 August 1968: Land Clearance, Operation Lyre Bird

*Typical: land clearance* · 1 Fd Sqn in contact

Australian: 1 wounded.

The land-clearing team of 1 Field Squadron was working around the Nui Thi Vai and Nui Dinh mountains when two enemy fired a rocket-propelled grenade and rifles at a D8 bulldozer, setting it alight and wounding the driver in the head, chest and legs. The engineers could not put the fire out, so pushed the bulldozer into a cutting and buried it; it was recovered to Nui Dat the next day.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=1441) · Report's sources: AWM95 7-4-30.pdf pg15-16; AWM95 7-4-31.pdf pg137 Contact Rept; Intsum, AWM95 1/4/113 pt3.pdf p3;

### 24 May 1969: Transport

*Typical: transport* · 1 Fd Sqn in contact

Australian: 3 wounded.

Two bulldozers of 1 Field Squadron set off three M16 mines at the edge of a village south-east of Long Dien. Three Australians were wounded, including a driver and a soldier walking behind, and the bulldozers were slightly damaged.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=2424) · Report's sources: Intsum, AWM95 1/4/152.pdf p139; AWM95 7-9-16.pdf 9RAR May 69 pg198-99

### 21 October 1969: Contact, Operation Backbone

*Typical: mine incident* · 1 Fd Sqn in contact

Australian: 1 killed, 1 wounded.

A small team of 1 Field Squadron clearing a road block on Route 326, working with B Squadron, 1 Armoured Regiment, set off a mine or booby trap. One Australian was killed and one wounded.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=3036) · Report's sources: Sitrep, AWM95 1/4/170.pdf p69

### 9 November 1969: Contact

*Significant* · 1 Fd Sqn in contact

Australian: 4 wounded.

The mine-clearing team of 1 Field Squadron set off an M16 mine 300 metres north of Phuoc Loi. Four Australians were wounded.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=3110) · Report's sources: Sitrep, AWM95 1/4/173.pdf p71; AWM95 2-2-10.pdf pg52 (Sitrep)

### 22 April 1970: Contact, Operation Concrete

*Significant* · 1 Fd Sqn in contact

Australian: 1 killed, 4 wounded.

Engineers of 1 Field Squadron and A Company, 6 RAR/NZ, set off an M16 mine on a bullock track into a clearing in the jungle seven kilometres south of Xuyen Moc, after at least four men had already walked over it. One Australian was killed and four wounded. The mine had been laid after 8 RAR left the area, beside a Viet Cong marker of a broken branch; the company commander warned that clearings likely to be used as landing zones, and the tracks into them, should be expected to be mined.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=3768) · Report's sources: AWM109 Ops 1ATF \[C\]; Intsum, AWM95 1/4/186.pdf p93

### 11 April 1971: Contact, Operation Briar Patch 1

*Last* · 1 Fd Sqn in contact

A mine-clearing tank of 1 Field Squadron set off a 60- to 80-pound mine in the jungle off Route 328, which badly damaged the tank. It could not be determined whether the mine was command- or pressure-detonated.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=4463) · Report's sources: Intsum, AWM95 1/4/219.pdf p132
