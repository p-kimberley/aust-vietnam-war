### 10 January 1969: Bunker, Operation Goodwood

*Significant* · 9 RAR in contact

Australian: 1 killed, 6 wounded. Enemy: 10 killed.

3 Platoon, A Company, 9 RAR, sweeping an area, came under fire from a large bunker system with heavy and light machine-guns, rocket-propelled grenades and Claymores. The fight drew in 2 Platoon and lasted three and a half hours, until contact was broken at dusk with the help of artillery, mortars and helicopter gunships. One Australian was killed and six wounded; ten enemy were killed.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=1900) · Report's sources: Intsum, AWM95 1/4/136.pdf p92; AWM95 7-9-8.pdf 9 RAR Jan 69, Contact Rept pg32

### 18 January 1969: Patrol, Operation Goodwood

*Typical: patrol* · 9 RAR in contact

Australian: 1 killed, 2 wounded. Enemy: 2 killed, 3 wounded.

2 Platoon, A Company, 9 RAR, was engaged from the rear by about ten enemy in black and green, who withdrew. Two enemy were killed and three wounded, and rifles and seven packs of rice captured; one Australian was shot in the stomach and two others slightly hurt.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=1935) · Report's sources: Intsum, 1/4/136.pdf p117; AWM95 7-9-9.pdf 9 RAR Jan 69 pg178, 183; AWM95 7-9-10.pdf 9 RAR Jan 69 pg205

### 19 January 1969: Security, Operation Goodwood

*Significant* · 9 RAR in contact

Australian: 3 killed, 5 wounded.

A Company, 9 RAR, was packing up its Claymores to leave its night position when three rocket-propelled grenades were fired at it; one landed among a section of 2 Platoon gathered for orders. Three Australians were killed and five wounded. The platoon returned fire and called in artillery and gunships, and the lesson drawn was not to stand around in groups.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=1943) · Report's sources: Intsum, AWM95 1/4/136.pdf p119; AWM95 7-9-8.pdf 9 RAR Jan 69, Contact Rept pg41

### 4 February 1969: Ambush, Operation Goodwood

*Typical: ambush* · 9 RAR in contact

Enemy: 6 killed, 2 wounded.

5 Platoon, B Company, 9 RAR, ambushed a creek crossing at first light as about 20 enemy came in quietly and well spread out. Claymores and rifle fire killed three, three more were killed as the rest tried to escape, and artillery and mortars hit the escape routes; six enemy were killed and more later found dead of shrapnel wounds. The platoon commander credited the success to concealment and fire discipline, and to using all its firepower once the ambush was sprung.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=2016) · Report's sources: AWM95 7-9-11.pdf 9 RAR Feb 69 Contact Rept pg27; 9 RAR Hist pg46; AWM95 7-9-11.pdf 9 RAR Feb 69 pg111;Intsum, AWM95 1/4/140.pdf p66

### 8 February 1969: Bunker, Operation Goodwood

*Significant* · In 5 RAR's contact

Australian: 1 killed, 5 wounded. Enemy: 4 killed.

D Company, 9 RAR, moving west, ran into a bunker system of 21 bunkers that was bigger than it first thought. 11 Platoon was hit by rocket-propelled grenades, and when the medical helicopter came in, 12 Platoon was fired on by heavy machine-guns and rockets and the helicopter was driven off; the company withdrew while artillery, air strikes and gunships hit the camp, and found it empty the next day. One Australian was killed and five wounded; four enemy bodies were found. The company commander warned against being drawn into a bunker fight without heavy support, and noted how patiently the enemy held fire until a helicopter was hovering.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=2038) · Report's sources: Intsum, AWM95 1/4/140.pdf p73; AWM 290 item 9/25 op. Goodwood; AWM95 7-9-11.pdf 9 RAR Feb 69 pg174-179; AWM95 7-9-12.pdf 9 RAR Feb 69 pg196 Contact Rept;

### 14 February 1969: Bunker, Operation Goodwood

*Significant* · 9 RAR in contact

Australian: 5 wounded. Enemy: 6 killed.

8 Platoon, C Company, 9 RAR, entering a gully, fired on an enemy soldier and found itself facing at least a platoon in a bunker system, under rifle and rocket fire. The company commander brought 9 Platoon round the north flank and used artillery to block the south, but 9 Platoon was also hit and pulled back to let the artillery work on the bunkers. After five hours, gunships covered the company's withdrawal and the area was shelled; the bunkers were destroyed the next day after the enemy had gone. Five Australians were wounded and six enemy killed; the enemy's fire, aimed about a metre above the ground, was noted as very well controlled.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=2059) · Report's sources: AWM279, 723/R5/56; AWM95 7-9-12.pdf Feb 69 pg10-15, 18, 21; Intsum, AWM95 1/4/140.pdf p83

### 23 February 1969: Security, Operation Federal

*Significant* · 9 RAR in contact

Enemy: 15 killed.

D Company, 9 RAR, saw rockets being fired from about 600 metres away. Artillery and the mortar platoon fired on the launch site, and shouting and running were heard. A patrol the next morning found seven enemy dead, six makeshift launchers and nine rockets, and more bodies were found in the area in the following days, fifteen in all.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=2078) · Report's sources: AWM95 7-9-12.pdf Feb 69 pg86-88; AWM95 7-9-12.pdf 9 RAR Feb 69 pg202 Contact Rept.

### 8 April 1969: Bunker, Operation Overlander

*Typical: bunker* · 9 RAR in contact

Australian: 1 killed, 6 wounded. Enemy: 1 wounded.

12 Platoon, D Company, 9 RAR, came on a latrine and was fired on from an enemy platoon's bunker system, with snipers, a Claymore and rocket-propelled grenades, along a 200-metre front with good fire lanes. It withdrew by fire and movement under gunship, artillery and mortar cover, and the whole company then cleared 25 bunkers of a larger system by evening. One Australian died of wounds and six were wounded; one enemy sniper was shot out of a tree. The platoon noticed the enemy's fire followed its machine-guns, and suggested moving them during a long fight.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=2227) · Report's sources: Intsum AWM95 1/4/148.pdf p106; 9 RAR Hist pg56; AWM95 7-9-14.pdf 9 RAR Apr 69 pg7, 97-107; AWM95 7-9-15.pdf 9 RAR Apr 69 Contact Rept pg195

### 6 June 1969: Security, Operation Reynella

*Typical: security* · 9 RAR in contact

Australian: 1 killed, 7 wounded.

Fire Support Base Thrust, 9 RAR's headquarters, was hit by 25 to 30 mortar bombs. The enemy fired while the base's own guns were firing, which made their mortar hard to locate; it was found by a patrol the next day. One Australian was killed and six or seven wounded.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=2492) · Report's sources: Sitrep, AWM95 1/4/156.pdf p15; AWM95 7-9-17.pdf 9RAR May 69 pg146; AWM95 7-9-18.pdf 9 RAR June 69 pg75

### 19 July 1969: Bunker, Operation Hat Dich

*Significant* · 9 RAR in contact

Australian: 1 killed, 8 wounded. Enemy: 4 wounded.

Following up an earlier contact, the forward section of 7 Platoon, C Company, 9 RAR, came under heavy rocket and rifle fire from about a platoon in a large bunker system. The forward scout was killed and eight men wounded, one of them trying to reach him, and the section lost its weapons as it was dragged clear. 9 Platoon's move round the flank eased the pressure; low cloud ruled out air strikes, but helicopter gunships were very effective, and artillery fired through the night. When the company attacked the next morning after heavy shelling, the enemy had gone, leaving blood trails.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=2692) · Report's sources: 9 RAR Hist pg82-3; Intsum, AWM95 1/4/160.pdf p135; AWM95 7-9-20, 9RAR Jul 69 pg27-28, 38

### 4 September 1969: Security, Operation Nepabunna

*Significant* · In 1 ATF's contact

Australian: 4 wounded. Enemy: 1 killed, 8 wounded.

The enemy got through the Regional Force bunkers into Dat Do and came up against a small mobile advisory team from B Company, 9 RAR, firing rocket-propelled grenades. The team fought back with a gunship aircraft overhead through the night, and tanks, carriers and a platoon were assembled to attack at dawn, but the enemy withdrew at first light. Three or four Australians were wounded; four Regional Force soldiers were killed and four wounded, and one enemy was killed.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=2885) · Report's sources: 9 RAR Hist pg91-2; Sitrep AWM 98 (313) Part 1; AWM95 7-9-23.pdf 9 RAR Aug 69 pg37; Intsum, AWM95 1/4/167.pdf p168

### 24 October 1969: Ambush, Operation JACK

*Significant* · 9 RAR in contact

Enemy: 6 killed, 6 wounded.

A Company ambushed a large enemy group of 50 to 60. 1 Platoon let a leading party of five through, and 2 Platoon, 200 metres away, let the vanguard pass and sprang its ambush on the next group of about 20; artillery and a gunship aircraft then fired on the larger group behind. Three enemy were possibly killed and six wounded, and 100 pounds of rice was recovered.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=3048) · Report's sources: Sitrep, AWM95 1/4/170.pdf p79; AWM109 Ops 1ATF \[J\]; 9 RAR Hist pg98; AWM95 7-9-26.pdf 9 RAR Oct 69 pg19, 20;

### 14 November 1969: Security

*Last* · 9 RAR in contact

A patrol of 9 RAR heard about three Viet Cong moving to its west, and fired pre-arranged defensive fire on the area with unknown results.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=5911) · Report's sources: Intsum, AWM95 1/4/173.pdf p177
