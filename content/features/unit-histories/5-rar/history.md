### 3 March 1966: Contact

*First* · 5 RAR in contact

1 Platoon of A Company, 5 RAR, made contact with five enemy soldiers moving south. The result was not known.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=2) · Report's sources: Intel V-dat Base

### 21 February 1967: Contact, Operation Renmark

*Significant* · In 3 Cav Regt's contact

Australian: 9 killed, 26 wounded.

B Company, 5 RAR, was moving by armoured personnel carrier towards the Long Hai Hills for a Task Force attack when the leading carrier hit a powerful mine, killing two cavalrymen and two infantrymen and badly wounding the rest aboard. Men who ran to help the wounded set off more anti-personnel mines, killing or wounding most of the medics and company headquarters, until engineers ordered everyone to keep still while they cleared the area. Nine Australians were killed and 26 wounded, and Operation Renmark was cancelled.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=452) · Report's sources: AWM279 569/R4/37; When the Scorpion Stings pg55; On the Offensive pg121; AWM95 7-5-15.pdf 5 RAR Mar 67 pg158;

### 8 February 1969: Bunker, Operation Goodwood

*Typical: bunker* · 5 RAR in contact

Australian: 1 killed, 5 wounded. Enemy: 4 killed.

D Company, 9 RAR, moving west, ran into a bunker system of 21 bunkers that was bigger than it first thought. 11 Platoon was hit by rocket-propelled grenades, and when the medical helicopter came in, 12 Platoon was fired on by heavy machine-guns and rockets and the helicopter was driven off; the company withdrew while artillery, air strikes and gunships hit the camp, and found it empty the next day. One Australian was killed and five wounded; four enemy bodies were found. The company commander warned against being drawn into a bunker fight without heavy support, and noted how patiently the enemy held fire until a helicopter was hovering.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=2038) · Report's sources: Intsum, AWM95 1/4/140.pdf p73; AWM 290 item 9/25 op. Goodwood; AWM95 7-9-11.pdf 9 RAR Feb 69 pg174-179; AWM95 7-9-12.pdf 9 RAR Feb 69 pg196 Contact Rept;

### 27 March 1969: Transport, Operation Federal

*Significant* · In 3 Cav Regt's contact

Australian: 20 wounded.

A Company, 5 RAR, with A Squadron, 3 Cavalry Regiment, was escorting a convoy of trucks up Route 15 in Bien Hoa when two US lorries hit mines 50 metres apart. About 20 Australians were wounded, half of whom stayed on duty, and two Americans.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=2162) · Report's sources: Intsum, AWM95 1/4/144.pdf p121; Scorpion Stings pg157; AWM95 7-5-20.pdf pg27, 7-5-21.pdf p128;

### 4 April 1969: Bunker, Operation Federal

*Significant* · 5 RAR in contact

Australian: 2 killed, 7 wounded. Enemy: 4 killed.

11 Platoon, D Company, 5 RAR, was spread along its objective when a platoon of enemy in a bunker system opened fire on all of it at once, leaving the commander no reserve to extract it. Claymores, heavy rifle fire and rocket-propelled grenades caused heavy casualties. 12 Platoon, helicopter gunships and air strikes came in, the artillery achieving little, and after four hours 11 Platoon got clear with its wounded. Two Australians were killed and seven wounded; four enemy bodies were later found in fresh graves.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=2208) · Report's sources: Intsum AWM95 1/4/148.pdf p94; AWM103, 569/2/155, Operations, 1ATF Particular, Operation Overlander. Contact Rept;

### 6 June 1969: Contact, Operation Hammer

*Significant* · 5 RAR in contact

Australian: 1 killed, 10 wounded. Enemy: 43 killed, 6 wounded.

The Battle of Binh Ba. B and D Companies of 5 RAR, with tanks of B Squadron, 1 Armoured Regiment, carriers of B Squadron, 3 Cavalry Regiment, and local Regional and Popular Force troops, fought an estimated 90 enemy in the village of Binh Ba from late morning until evening. One Australian was killed and eight to ten wounded, and two Centurion tanks were damaged; 27 enemy were counted dead, more were recorded later, and two were captured.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=2496) · Report's sources: Sitrep, AWM95 1/4/156.pdf p16; Analysis of Aust Urban Engagements in Vn; Scorpion Stings pg169-78;

### 7 June 1969: Contact, Operation Hammer/Tong

*Significant* · 5 RAR in contact

Enemy: 40 killed.

A company of the 33rd North Vietnamese Regiment attacked Duc Trung and overwhelmed its local defence platoon. B Company, 5 RAR, was sent to retake it: artillery fire was brought within 20 metres of the houses, helicopter gunships attacked the north and west of the village, and B Company attacked from the south with a troop of tanks. The enemy were routed, and local forces cleared the rest of the village to avoid civilian casualties. Forty enemy were recorded killed.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=2509) · Report's sources: AWM 279 723/R5/59; AWM95 7-5-25.pdf pg36; Analysis of Australian Urban Engagements in Vietnam, R Hall and A Ross, ADSC, Unsw@ADFA May 2003;

### 15 June 1969: Contact, Operation ESSO

*Significant* · 5 RAR in contact

Australian: 3 killed, 24 wounded.

A soldier of D Company, 5 RAR, stepped on an M16 mine on the southern edge of Dat Do; the mine jumped two metres before exploding. Three Australians were killed and 24 wounded. Another mine, laid only in the last few days, was found nearby, and the report notes the district chief had been told three days earlier that the company would be there.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=2547) · Report's sources: Intsum, AWM95 1/4/156.pdf p120; AWM95 7-5-25.pdf pg8,63,65,

### 16 June 1969: Security, Operation ESSO

*Typical: security* · 5 RAR in contact

Australian: 1 killed, 2 wounded. Enemy: 2 killed.

10 Platoon, D Company, 5 RAR, was fired on with rifles and rocket-propelled grenades by an enemy squad. One Australian was killed and two wounded; two to four enemy were killed and four weapons captured. The platoon seems to have stayed in one place too long and been spotted.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=2550) · Report's sources: Intsum, AWM95 1/4/156.pdf p121; Sitrep AWM 98 (323); AWM95 7-5-30.pdf pg169 Contact Rept;

### 24 June 1969: Patrol, Operation ESSO

*Typical: patrol* · 5 RAR in contact

Australian: 7 wounded. Enemy: 2 killed, 6 wounded.

A combined patrol of 10 Australians of 3 Platoon, A Company, 5 RAR, and 55 South Vietnamese soldiers, moving to a new ambush site, was attacked from all sides by a platoon of Viet Cong main-force troops. Poor radio contact delayed artillery until a spotter aircraft relayed the calls, and the enemy closed in to escape the shells, but two helicopter gunships fired within 25 metres of the patrol and the enemy withdrew. Seven South Vietnamese were wounded by rockets; two enemy were killed and possibly six more.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=2588) · Report's sources: AWM288 459/1/2/2 pt3 After Action Reports 5 RAR 66-69; AWM95 7-5-31.pdf pg10 Contact Rept;

### 4 July 1969: Patrol, Operation ESSO

*Significant* · 5 RAR in contact

Australian: 3 killed, 14 wounded.

C Company, 5 RAR, found a bunker system on the south-east edge of the Long Hai Hills with anti-vehicle mines, a Claymore and a command-detonated shell. That night 7 Platoon set off an M16 mine, and another went off while the wounded were being moved; a third was found nearby. Three Australians were killed and fourteen wounded, one of them an engineer.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=2633) · Report's sources: Intsum, AWM95 1/4/160.pdf p99; AWM95 7-5-29.pdf pg7,90-1

### 6 August 1969: Ambush, Operation Camden

*Typical: ambush* · 5 RAR in contact

Australian: 1 wounded. Enemy: 7 killed, 9 wounded.

12 Platoon, D Company, 5 RAR, was in ambush in two half-platoon groups 70 metres apart when about 45 enemy moved north along the track through both. Both groups opened fire, and the enemy withdrew hastily without firing back, dragging away bodies and wounded under fire. Gunships, artillery and mortars covered the area, and the sweep at first light found seven dead and captured rifles, Claymores, food and documents. One Australian was slightly wounded by a grenade and stayed on duty.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=2747) · Report's sources: Contact Report AWM288, item R459/1/2/2 Part 1, After action Reports

### 21 August 1969: Bunker, Operation Camden 2

*Significant* · 5 RAR in contact

Australian: 1 killed, 37 wounded. Enemy: 9 killed, 25 wounded.

A Company, 5 RAR's Assault Pioneer Platoon heard voices ahead and, with 3 Platoon, assaulted a bunker system after artillery fire behind it to cut off the enemy. They met very heavy rocket and rifle fire from the bunkers and flanks, and pulled back to a clearing to evacuate the wounded; as the medical helicopters arrived, the enemy closed in and fired mortars, rifles and rockets into the wounded, until helicopter gunships drove them back and the evacuation was moved. One Australian was killed and more than 30 wounded; nine enemy were recorded killed and 25 wounded. The company commander said the radio net had been chaotic with every supporting element on it.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=2833) · Report's sources: AWM 288, 569/1/112/2 Pt 2 Camden; Intsum, AWM95 1/4/164.pdf p164; Sitrep, AWM95 1/4/164.pdf p74-5; AWM95 7-5-32.pdf pg176 Contact Rept;

### 14 February 1970: Ambush, Operation Bondi

*Last* · 5 RAR in contact

Australian: 1 wounded. Enemy: 1 killed.

8 Platoon, C Company, 5 RAR, and company headquarters were ambushing a main track when the rear machine-gunner saw enemy coming along a footpad behind them and opened fire. One enemy was killed and the others escaped. One Australian was wounded when a Claymore shorted and went off.

[Open on the Battle Map](/battlemap?incident=3511) · Report's sources: Sitrep, AWM95 1/4/181.pdf Pt2 p45; AWM95 7-5-38.pdf pg177 Contact Rept;
